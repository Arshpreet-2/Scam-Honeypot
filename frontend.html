    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is it safe to open a scam link with this?

    Yes. The page is opened inside an isolated cloud browser, never on your device, and read-only — it looks at the page but never fills a form, pays, or enters credentials.

    How is this different from Google Safe Browsing or VirusTotal?

    Those check whether a URL is already known-bad. Scam Honeypot reaches the India-specific cloaked funnel they can’t load, pulls out the hidden payment account, and gives the citizen the real link.

    Does it name the scammer?

    No. It exposes the account and identifiers collecting the money and flags them to banks and I4C. Attaching a real person needs KYC/telecom data only the authorities can obtain.

    Is it accurate every time?

    No detector is perfect. Every verdict is a risk score with the reasons shown, so a person can judge it — not a black-box “100% scam”.
